## Owner decisions (2026-11-21)

- **One page, no sub-tabs.**
  - The **rate chart leads**; the KPI strip comes after it.
  - **No duplication of the Overview:** the Today box was removed from the Currency page. The Overview keeps its Currency card unchanged.
  - Only views the Overview does not have go on this page.
- **Quote follows the top EUR/USD switch** (no toggle on the chart):
  - EUR view: USD/EUR (one dollar in euros).
  - USD view: EUR/USD.
  - This is the same as the Today card's `today.pair`.
- **Chart text in three places only:** the headline, the legend, and one row of four same-shaped figures:
  - Low – high
  - Dollars you hold
  - Euros you convert
  - Dollar trend
- **Strip:** rows on phones, whole euros, and no rate or avg tiles (they are on the chart).
- **Overview trend hints:** both. A chip on the Today currency card, plus a sparkline and chip on the lifetime card.
